#### 🤸 **Desk Exercises**

```
🤸 5-MINUTE DESK WORKOUT

No equipment needed!

**1. Neck Stretch (30 sec)**
   - Slowly tilt head to left
   - Hold 10 seconds
   - Repeat right
   - Forward and back

**2. Shoulder Rolls (30 sec)**
   - Roll backwards 10 times
   - Roll forwards 10 times

**3. Wrist Circles (30 sec)**
   - Rotate wrists clockwise
   - Then counter-clockwise

**4. Seated Twist (30 sec each side)**
   - Sit straight
   - Twist torso left
   - Hold 15 sec
   - Repeat right

**5. Leg Extensions (1 min)**
   - Extend leg straight
   - Hold 5 seconds
   - Alternate legs

**6. Standing Calf Raises (1 min)**
   - Stand
   - Rise on toes
   - Lower slowly
   - Repeat 20 times

**7. Deep Breathing (1 min)**
   - Inhale 4 counts
   - Hold 4 counts
   - Exhale 6 counts
   - Repeat 5 times

Done! Feel better?
```

### Specific Health Issues

#### 😣 **Headache Relief**

```
😣 HEADACHE? LET'S HELP

Common desk work causes:

**1. Dehydration** (most common)
   → Drink 2 glasses of water NOW
   
**2. Eye Strain**
   → 20-20-20 rule + palm eyes
   
**3. Poor Posture**
   → Neck and shoulder stretch
   
**4. Tension**
   → Massage temples, deep breaths
   
**5. Caffeine Withdrawal**
   → Regular caffeine intake or quit gradually

**Quick Relief:**
- Dark room (reduce light)
- Cool compress on forehead
- Peppermint oil on temples
- 10-minute rest (eyes closed)

If persists > 2 hours: Take pain reliever and rest

**Prevention:**
All the above + regular breaks!
```
